Garlic Butter Steak & Sunny-Side-Up Eggs

Juicy flat iron steak, three golden-yolk eggs, and one teaspoon of butter. Simple, hearty, protein-forward.

High proteinOne skilletNo milk
5 minPrep
15 minCook
20 minTotal
1Serving
~499Calories
~51gProtein
~1gCarbs
~34gFat

What you'll need

1

Let's make it

  1. Season. Pat 6 oz raw flat iron steak dry. Season both sides with garlic powder, black pepper, and salt.
  2. Sear. Heat a nonstick or well-seasoned skillet over medium-high heat. Sear steak about 3–5 minutes per side, depending on thickness.
  3. Butter-baste. Lower heat and add 1 tsp Land O'Lakes butter. Spoon the melted butter over steak. For food safety, cook whole-cut steak to 145°F and rest 3 minutes.
  4. Sunny-side up. Move steak to a board. Lower heat to medium-low and crack three eggs into the same skillet, keeping yolks intact. Cover and cook approximately 2–4 minutes until whites are set. Runny yolks carry some food-safety risk; fully cooked eggs have firm yolks.
  5. Plate. Slice rested steak against the grain and serve alongside eggs, spooning remaining pan juices over steak.
Kitchen note: Butter is shared across the steak and eggs—no extra cooking fat or milk. Nutrition is estimated from the previously discussed ingredient labels; actual amounts vary by cut and cooking losses.

Strawberry Banana Protein Oatmeal & Shake

Warm banana oatmeal, creamy strawberry flavor, and the rest of the protein shake on the side.

What you'll need

Check off ingredients and scale the number of complete meals.

1

Let's make it

  1. Measure the shake. Empty one 30 g Quaker Bananas & Cream oatmeal packet into a roomy, microwave-safe bowl (at least 2-cup capacity). Measure ½ cup (120 mL / 4 fl oz) from the chilled 340 mL Member's Mark Strawberry Protein Shake. Reserve the rest to drink.
  2. Mix thoroughly. Stir the measured shake into the dry oats until all flakes are wet. Do not microwave the bottle or a sealed container.
  3. Use gentle microwave power. Set microwave to 50% power (Power Level 5 on many models), not full power. Heat uncovered for 30 seconds, then stop and stir well. Repeat in 20–30 second bursts, stirring after each, until oats are tender and mixture is hot, typically 1½–2½ minutes total heating depending on wattage and bowl.
  4. Prevent scorching or separation. Stop if the mixture starts bubbling rapidly or climbing the bowl; let it settle and stir before continuing. If too thick, stir in 1 tablespoon water at a time. Avoid boiling: milk proteins can separate or form a skin when overheated, though lower power cannot guarantee zero separation.
  5. Rest and serve. Let stand 1 minute and stir again; the oatmeal thickens as it rests. Drink the remaining ~220 mL (7.4 fl oz) shake cold alongside the bowl. The full shake is included in the meal macros.
Microwave cheat sheet ✦
50% power → 30 seconds → stir → 20–30 seconds → stir → repeat only as needed. Do not leave unattended or boil. If your microwave has no power-level setting, use 10–15 second bursts with stirring and cooling pauses instead.
For the two of you ♡
Make two bowls separately for more even heating, and use one whole shake per person. Two meals = two oatmeal packets + two shake bottles.

Macros use the two labels provided: oatmeal 110 kcal / 3g P / 22g C / 1.5g F; full shake 160 kcal / 30g P / 3g C / 2.5g F. No extra milk or toppings. Heating time is approximate and varies by microwave. Milk-based shakes may still develop a skin or slight graininess when heated.

Garlic & Herb Rotisserie Chicken Protein+ Pasta

Buttery garlic. Savory herbs. A little Parmesan magic.

Let's make it

  1. Cook the penne. Bring salted water to a boil. Cook Barilla Protein+ Penne according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup starchy pasta water, then drain.
  2. Build the garlic-butter base. Melt butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add minced garlic and stir 60–90 seconds until fragrant, not brown.
  3. Wake up the herbs. Add Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, and black pepper. Stir 30 seconds.
  4. Toss everything together. Add shredded rotisserie chicken and drained pasta. Add about ½ cup reserved pasta water. Toss over low heat until the chicken is thoroughly heated.
  5. Finish silky. Turn off the heat. Add finely grated Parmigiano Reggiano gradually, tossing as you go. Add more pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce coats the penne. Taste and adjust salt.
  6. Meal prep like a pro. Weigh the finished dish (without the pot), divide by 8, and portion into containers. Refrigerate up to 3–4 days; freeze extras promptly.
Chef's little secret ✦
Starchy pasta water turns a modest amount of butter, olive oil and Parmesan into a silky coating. Reheat leftovers with a splash of water to bring the sauce back to life.
For the two of you ♡
Make dinner a mini date: one person handles the garlic and herbs, the other shreds chicken and grates Parmesan. Then taste-test together before packing portions.
